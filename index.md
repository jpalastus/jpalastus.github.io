    <h1>João Paulo Almeida de Mendonça</h1>
    <p class="hero-role">Chargé de recherche CNRS · SIMaP, Grenoble, France (He/Him)</p>
    <p>
      I work in <strong>computational physics</strong>, applying it to <strong>materials science</strong>
      (and to complex systems, mostly for fun). A large part of my research involves
      <strong>bioinspired algorithms</strong> (evolutionary and particle-swarm methods) and
      <strong>machine-learning approaches</strong> (ANNs, clustering, manifold learning), combined with
      classical and first-principles simulation tools (<em>LAMMPS</em>, <em>VASP</em>, <em>xTB</em>,
      <em>ORCA</em>, <em>PySCF</em>…).
    </p>
    <p>
      At <a href="https://simap.grenoble-inp.fr/en/">SIMaP</a>, I develop
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00600">machine-learning density functionals</a> to explore
      potential energy surfaces with DFT, with a focus on transition-metal complexes and nanoclusters. I am also
      part of the <a href="https://diamond-diadem.github.io/en/">DIAMOND project</a> (PEPR DIADEM), where I use to built
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/adem.202503185">reproducible container solutions and machine-learning workflows</a>
      for materials science.
    </p>
