## Research areas

<div class="grid-2">
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Machine learning for materials</h2>
    <p>
      Development of <strong>neural-network density functionals</strong> for DFT, with applications to
      <strong>spin-crossover transition-metal complexes</strong> and adiabatic energy differences
      (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00600">JCTC 2023</a>,
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01291">JCTC 2025</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Bioinspired &amp; global optimization</h2>
    <p>
      Evolutionary algorithms, particle-swarm optimization and custom bioinspired methods
      (e.g. the <a href="https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1906.11375">Frogs method</a> for micromagnetic skyrmion data extraction) 
      to locate global minima of potential-energy surfaces of <strong>finite-size particles and nanoclusters</strong>,
      including mixed-metal clusters for catalysis
      (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-024-01726-3">Nat. Chem. 2025</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Atomistic simulation of materials</h2>
    <p>
      Classical and <em>ab initio</em> molecular dynamics plus quantum chemistry (DFT, CCSD, semiempirical)
      applied to <strong>metals, alloys and metal oxides</strong> (bulk and clusters),
      <strong>2D materials</strong> (graphene, graphene oxide, TMDs), <strong>transition-metal complexes</strong>
      and <strong>MOFs</strong>.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Reproducible research software</h2>
    <p>
      Within the <a href="https://diamond-diadem.github.io/en/">DIAMOND project</a> (PEPR DIADEM), I work on
      <strong>reproducible container solutions</strong> (GNU Guix + Apptainer) and ML workflows for materials
      science (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/adem.202503185">Adv. Eng. Mater. 2026</a>), aiming at FAIR
      codes and workflows shared with the community.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Complex systems (for fun)</h2>
    <p>
      Dynamical systems, cellular automata and agent-based models — from skyrmion dynamics in nanotracks to
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s00283-019-09893-9">modeling a hypothetical zombie outbreak</a> as a
      playful way to teach epidemiology and modeling.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Scientific illustration</h2>
    <p>
      I also like to produce vector/raster artwork and 3D renders for science communication — journal covers, lab logos
      and event identities. A selection is in the <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/gallery.html">Gallery</a>.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
